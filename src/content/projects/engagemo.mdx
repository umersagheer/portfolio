## Overview

A full-stack writing education platform for schools and independent teachers,
with 30–40 institutions onboard. Students write, an AI coach gives feedback, and
teachers run their classrooms: assignments, grading, progress and reports, with
subscription-based feature controls.

## What I built

- Role-based platform for students, teachers and admins, with secure auth flows
- Bulk AI feedback processing on BullMQ + Redis queues, with retries and live
  progress updates
- OCR for image and PDF submissions using Google Cloud Vision
- Automated individual and class reports with AI analysis and downloadable PDF
  export
- Realtime notifications and report status updates over Socket.IO
- Stripe subscription billing with feature gating (AI usage, OCR access,
  institutional limits)
