## Overview

A full-stack mobile app that helps small business owners understand true service
profitability from labour, overhead, capacity and AI-powered recommendations. An
Expo React Native client runs on a NestJS and PostgreSQL backend.

## What I built

- Clerk authentication with protected app flows and secure webhook-based user
  sync
- Guided onboarding with industry presets, suggested roles, and overhead setup
  (simple or categorised)
- A service profitability engine that calculates true cost, margin, profit per
  hour and the minimum safe price
- Automatic recalculation of every related service when role rates or overhead
  change
- An AI insights pipeline on LangChain and LangGraph with OpenAI, with
  queue-based generation and report history
- Realtime AI generation status over Socket.IO, and a RevenueCat paywall for
  locked insight reports
