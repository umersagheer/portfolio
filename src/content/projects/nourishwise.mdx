## Overview

A mobile-first postpartum wellness app built with React Native and Expo. It
works out personal calorie and macro targets during onboarding, then supports
the day with meal planning, mood and nutrition tracking, recipes, groceries and
community forums, plus premium subscriptions. Supabase handles auth, behind a
typed API service layer.

## What I built

- Supabase authentication with email and password, plus Google, Apple and
  Facebook sign-in
- Dynamic onboarding that calculates personalised postpartum calorie and macro
  targets
- A daily dashboard with a calendar, mood logging, recommendations, a nutrition
  graph and meal cards
- A recipe system with search, favourites, user-created recipes, and nutrition
  calculated before publishing
- Grocery management with add, edit and delete, merged duplicates, and a
  recipe-to-grocery flow
- Analytics for nutrition, mood trends, streaks, badges and engagement
- Community forums with posts, comments, likes, filters, and follow and follower
  interactions
- RevenueCat premium subscriptions with feature gating and in-app plan
  management
