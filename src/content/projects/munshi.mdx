## Overview

Munshi is an all-in-one restaurant system built for Pakistan. Orders, the
kitchen, stock, staff, cash and _khata_ (running credit for regulars and for
suppliers) live in one app, from a first counter to a tenth branch.

It keeps taking orders when the internet drops, prints kitchen tickets, bills
and tokens, and shows Urdu menu names next to the English ones.

## RFC, the first customer

RFC serves karahi, BBQ, burgers and pizza on Main Faisalabad–Lahore Road in
Manawala. It was the first restaurant to run on Munshi.

- **10,500+** orders taken on Munshi
- **163** orders on its busiest day
- **9** staff on attendance and payroll
- Running on Munshi since **September 2025**

**Before.** Orders went through one system, stock lived in a register, and
salaries were worked out by hand at the end of the month. Every report meant
collecting numbers from three places, and a short cash drawer was found weeks
later, if at all.

**Switching over.** Munshi was built alongside RFC's own counter and kitchen, so
every screen was tested in a real rush before it shipped. Staff moved over one
area at a time: the counter first, then the kitchen tickets, then purchases and
payroll.

**Now.** Orders, kitchen tickets, purchases, attendance and the day book all run
in one place. The drawer is counted every night, supplier balances update
themselves, and salaries come out of the attendance the staff already mark.

## What it does

- **Orders:** dine-in, takeaway and delivery, with payments, bills and kitchen
  tickets (including modification tickets)
- **Kitchen:** a live display, updated across every screen as orders change
- **Stock and purchases:** products, variants, recipes and ingredients, stock
  intake, adjustments and waste
- **Staff:** roles you design, attendance, and payroll worked out from it
- **Cash and khata:** opening and closing the drawer, expenses, and running
  balances for regulars and suppliers
- **Reports:** sales, menu items and branches
- **Branches:** each outlet runs on its own, managers see only theirs, owners
  see all of them
- **Online ordering:** a page for each restaurant on its own subdomain

## Under the hood

A Next.js app on PostgreSQL through Prisma. Socket.IO keeps the counter, kitchen
and admin screens in sync.

The app works offline, so a dropped connection doesn't stop the counter:

- a Serwist service worker serves the app
- the TanStack Query cache is persisted
- writes made offline go into an outbox in IndexedDB (Dexie) and replay when the
  connection returns

Each replayed write carries an idempotency key, so a resend is recognised
instead of applied twice.

Staff sign in with a PIN, and every outlet has its own role-based permissions.

## What's next

The restaurant system is the first in a family of management systems. Grocery
management is next.
